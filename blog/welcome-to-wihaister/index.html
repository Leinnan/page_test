Welcome to Wihaister

An introduction to the future of remote desktop access.

What is Wihaister?

Wihaister creates a direct, encrypted link between you and your machine. It punches through firewalls and NATs so distance never slows you down.

Peer-to-Peer Architecture

Connections are peer-to-peer. Relay servers are only used as an encrypted fallback — they cannot read your data. Every session is encrypted end-to-end over QUIC.

Performance

  • 4K resolution at 60 FPS
  • Connect to any monitor on your workstation
  • Full touch input support

Platform Support

Wihaister is available on Windows and macOS today. Linux support is planned, and iOS and Android clients are in active development.
