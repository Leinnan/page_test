How Wihaister Protects Your Data

Wihaister is built on a foundation of privacy-first design. Every connection is encrypted from end to end, and we never have access to your traffic.

End-to-End Encryption

All traffic is encrypted over QUIC, a modern transport protocol designed for speed and security. The encryption keys are generated and held only by the two endpoints in a session — your device and your workstation.

Relay Architecture

When a direct peer-to-peer path cannot be established, Wihaister uses relay servers as an encrypted fallback. These relays forward encrypted bytes — they cannot decrypt or read your data. Not even we can see what you’re doing.

Key Principles

  • No plaintext traffic ever leaves your machine
  • Encryption keys are never shared with relay servers
  • Each session generates fresh encryption keys
